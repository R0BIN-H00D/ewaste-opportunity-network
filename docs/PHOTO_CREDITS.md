# Photo credits

The demo devices and shop products show real photographs from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/), all under free licences that allow reuse **with credit**. This page is that credit. The photos live in `backend/seed-assets/real-photos/` and are put on the demo data by `npm run seed:realphotos` (in `backend/`). Each picture was resized and set on a soft background of itself so they all fit the app's 4:3 cards; nothing else was changed.

Photos that people upload to the app themselves are theirs and are never replaced.

Where Commons has no photo of the exact model, a photo of a very close one is used, and the table says so under *Shows*.
